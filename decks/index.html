LEGO

China Brand Positioning

Objective

Develop a positioning strategy for LEGO to earn more time and space in the lives of Chinese families and increase active share among school-age kids.

Approach

This presentation was the culmination of a research and strategy project conducted between McCann Worldgroup and LEGO Shanghai, arriving at one overarching positioning truth that would cascade across all LEGO business units. My role was lead project architect and strategist, and I designed the process as well as the final presentation.

Results

We developed a strategic roadmap to explore how LEGO could move beyond being a toy brand to a partner of Chinese families to explore and build. In subsequent years, LEGO enjoyed double digit growth in China.

Mercedes-Benz

AMG Motorsports Night Creative Presentation

Objective

Formula 1 Grand Prix parties are an important moment for automotive brands to inspire loyalty in their dealers and customers. Our goal was to wow the client with our creative vision for the event with an undeniably cool creative vision and detailed production plan.

Approach

The concept for the event was fusion of future and past, and art direction was key to communicate how this would come to life in a cohesive way. We approached the deck like a user journey, introducing our concept and then following guests from the moment they arrive until the end of the night.

Results

The client was so thrilled with the presentation that they skipped the process of producing detailed renders for each creative line item, giving more time and budget for production.

Takeda Pharmaceutical

All Employee Meeting

Objective

Takeda was producing their first in-person all employee meeting since COVID, with a new general manager on a mission to inspire confidence in their strategy and celebrate accomplishments.

Approach

This project was much more than a deck. Three days of presentations, guest speakers, and activities, all wrapped under a unifying event brand, Takeda Elevate. I conceptualized and designed the event branding as well as extension across touch points, worked with our video team to develop stand-alone videos and presentation transitions, worked with the C suite on their individual presentations, and planned the entire multi-day experience. Our goal was to create an immersive experience of reaching new heights, to literally elevate the audience with stunning wraparound visuals.

Results

Post-event survey results were highly positive across the board!

Tonic DNA

AI Innovation Webinar

Objective

When generative AI tools hit the market, advertising clients (and their agencies) began to question their production budgets. As an animation studio with a reputation for professionalism, we knew that we needed to act fast and establish our expertise in this new domain.

Approach

How do we know whether AI is the best option unless we test it for ourselves? We established that our studio was conducting ongoing research and development around AI and substantiated our position with interesting tests (that are already totally out-of-date). I designed and presented this webinar to an audience of Canadian agencies recruited through our rep.

Results

Over 60 decision-makers attended the webinar, and we won several experimental projects on the back of the presentation.

Tonic DNA

Ad Agency Presentation

Objective

Animation studio Tonic DNA needed to refresh their image with ad agencies in Canada. We asked our agency reps in Toronto to set up meetings with agency contacts for a general credentials presentation to spark new interest and win business.

Approach

Tonic DNA was celebrating our 40th anniversary, why not use that milestone and play against expectations by showcasing a persistent spirit of reinvention? More than just sell our services, we wanted to tell an inspiring story about creativity and change.

Results

With just a handful of presentations across a 30-day period, the effort led to three project inquiries and a renewed relationship with one major ad agency.
