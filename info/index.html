Jay Mark Caplan is a creative strategist with over 15 years of experience helping brands and businesses solve high-stakes problems.

His career has spanned strategy, creative, and production industries to bring a holistic perspective and unique set of skills to every project. Apart from strategic consulting, Jay runs an illustration studio. His drawings have appeared on bottles for Canadian brewer Collective Arts Brewery and sold-out merchandise for local indie bookstore Librairie Saint-Henri Books.

Based in Montreal, Jay loves comic books, skateboarding, rock climbing, and cooking for his wife and daughter.
